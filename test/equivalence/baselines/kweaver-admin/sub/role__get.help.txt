Usage: kweaver-admin role get [options] <role>

Show a role's details (resource type scopes, operations). Accepts a UUID or
exact role name; names resolve via `/api/authorization/v1/roles?keyword=...`.

Arguments:
  role           Role UUID or exact role name

Options:
  --view <mode>  resource_type_view_mode: flat (default) | hierarchy
  -h, --help     display help for command
